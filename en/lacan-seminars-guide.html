Lacan’s Seminars: Where Each Stands in 2026

Haktan Tay · 20 October 2026 · 4 min read

Which of Lacan’s seminars can be read in which language, and in which edition, is one of the first questions facing anyone who works on Lacan. This page gathers in a single table the French, English and Turkish publication status of the twenty-seven seminars as of autumn 2026, and adds a few notes on how to read it.

This page first appeared in Turkish. The English version is my own. The Turkish column is kept for readers interested in Lacan’s reception in Turkish.

I

Lacan’s seminar began in the autumn of 1953 at the Sainte-Anne hospital and ended in 1980 with the dissolution of the École freudienne de Paris. The seminars are published in Paris by Seuil, in the series Le Champ freudien, in texts established by Jacques-Alain Miller; since 2023 a new volume has appeared at the start of each year. How the text of the seminars was established, and its relation to the stenography and the parallel transcriptions, is discussed in detail in the essay “The Established Text” on this site; the history of the Turkish translations in the essay “Lacan in Turkish”.1

II

The table shows several things at a glance. First, twenty of the twenty-seven seminars have been published as books in French; three exist only in the pages of the journal Ornicar?, in the form Miller established; four, the ninth, twenty-first, twenty-fifth and twenty-sixth, have not been published in any official form. For these the researcher must rely on transcriptions based on the stenography.

Second, English translation lags behind French publication, but at an accelerating pace. Since 2014 Polity Press has published a series of volumes, most of them translated by A. R. Price, Bruce Fink and Russell Grigg. Of the twenty seminars available as books in French, seventeen can now be read in English; the translation of the fifteenth has been announced. The twelfth and thirteenth seminars, following their French publication in 2025 and 2026, have not yet been translated into English.

Third, only four seminars have been translated into Turkish as books: the first, second, eleventh and twentieth. For a reader of Lacan in Turkish these four volumes cover the first years of the teaching and a cross-section of its late period; but none of the seminars of the great theoretical years between 1956 and 1963, those of the graph of desire, the Name-of-the-Father, transference and anxiety, is yet available in Turkish.

The table will be updated on this site as new translations and editions appear. Readers who think a detail is wrong or missing are warmly invited to write, with their source, to psikologhaktantay@gmail.com; this is the greatest contribution to the table’s accuracy.
