Privacy & Cookies

About this translationThis is an English translation of the Turkish page, provided for information. In case of any discrepancy, the Turkish version prevails.

This page explains the cookies, third-party services and privacy practices of the haktantay.com website. For the full personal data protection policy covering the online psychological services, please see the KVKK page.

1. Use of cookies

This site does not use its own analytics cookies, and it contains no other analytics tool, advertising pixel or tracking tool. The one exception is the Google tag (gtag.js) added to the pages to measure Google Ads campaigns. This tag may use Google’s own cookies and identifiers; details are in section 2.

The site itself uses your browser’s localStorage only for the following technical purpose:

KeyPurposeDuration
kvkk_consentSo that the cookie notice is not shown againUntil you delete it

2. Third-party services

Google Ads tag (advertising measurement)

A Google tag (gtag.js; ID: AW-18172496319) has been added to the pages of the site to measure Google Ads campaigns (conversion tracking). The tag is loaded from googletagmanager.com. When it loads, Google may place its own cookies or similar identifiers in your browser and may receive data such as the page you visit, your IP address, and your device and browser information. This data is processed by Google LLC under its own privacy policy and may be transferred abroad, including to the United States. The choice you make in the cookie notice banner only stops the notice from appearing again; it does not control whether this tag loads. You can disable the tag with your browser’s tracking protection or a content blocker, and manage your preferences through Google’s ad settings.
Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Google Fonts (typography)

The site loads the Cormorant Garamond, DM Sans and (on essay pages) EB Garamond fonts from fonts.googleapis.com. On your first visit, your browser’s IP address therefore reaches Google’s servers.
Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Google Forms (contact form)

Data you send through the contact form is transmitted to Google’s servers via docs.google.com and stored in a linked Google Sheet. Form data is used only to reply to you.
Details: KVKK / Transfers to third parties

YouTube (videos in essays)

Some essays include official trailers and scenes as YouTube videos. These videos are not loaded when the page opens; they load only when you click the play button, from youtube-nocookie.com, Google’s privacy-enhanced mode. The moment you click, your browser connects to YouTube’s servers.
Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

GitHub Pages (hosting)

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Server access logs (IP, browser, requested page, timestamp) are kept by GitHub.
Policy: GitHub Privacy Statement

3. Resetting your cookie choice

You can make your cookie choice again through your browser’s developer tools:

  • Keyboard shortcut: F12 (or Cmd+Option+I)
  • Tab: Application → Storage → Local Storage
  • Find and delete the kvkk_consent key
  • Reload the page — the cookie notice will appear again

4. Security

  • All connections are encrypted over HTTPS
  • A strict Content Security Policy (CSP) is applied — only trusted sources can be loaded
  • Modern security headers: HSTS, X-Content-Type-Options, Referrer-Policy, Permissions-Policy
  • The contact form goes through Google’s spam filter

5. Requests for sessions through the site

For detailed information on the processing of data you send through the contact form, see the Privacy Notice and the KVKK Policy.

Important — sharing sensitive information Please do not share detailed clinical information through the contact form. This channel is only for a first contact and scheduling. An end-to-end encrypted online platform is used for the content of sessions.

6. Children’s privacy

The site’s content is intended for users aged 18 and over. Requests for sessions from people under 18 are considered only with the written consent of a parent or guardian.

7. Policy updates

This policy may be updated when necessary. Significant changes are announced on the home page. We recommend that you review the current version regularly.
